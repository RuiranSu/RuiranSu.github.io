About Me

I am a DPhil student in Engineering Science at the University of Oxford, supervised by Prof. Janet B. Pierrehumbert. As a Jardine Scholar (1.5% acceptance rate), my research focuses on developing trustworthy AI systems that integrate natural language processing, multimodal reasoning, and large language models to enhance factual reliability and decision-making in complex real-world domains.

Research Focus

My work specializes in the intersection of Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and Large Language Models (LLMs) to address the pressing issue of misinformation, particularly in climate science. I develop advanced NLP tools to assist online moderators and fact-checkers in identifying and mitigating false claims on social media platforms.

Research Projects

ClimateViz: Benchmark for Climate Fact-Checking

ClimateViz is a cutting-edge dataset developed to tackle misinformation in climate science. Sourced from reputable organizations such as NOAA, UK Met Office, and Copernicus, this dataset includes scientific graphics and visual data enriched with factual annotations. ClimateViz bridges the gap between visual and textual data, enabling researchers to evaluate and fine-tune large vision-language models (LVLMs) for enhanced fact-checking and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

Key Features:

  • Multi-source authoritative climate data visualization
  • Comprehensive factual annotations for fact-checking
  • Benchmark for vision-language model evaluation
  • Applications in misinformation detection and science communication

Graph Neural Networks for Social Media Analysis

Developing GNN-based approaches to understand climate discourse on social media platforms. This research integrates textual and social network data to analyze discussions, identify misinformation spreaders, and understand the dynamics of climate-related debates online.

Intent-Based Networking with Knowledge Graphs

Research on Intent-based Networks (IBNs) empowered by Knowledge Graphs for next-generation telecommunication systems. This work integrates knowledge graph technology with IBN to improve translation and management of user intents, with applications in smart grid scenarios and 6G networks.
